About Genious IAS

A Beacon of Excellence in Civil Services Coaching

Genious IAS is a premier coaching institute located in Rohtak, specialising in preparing students for the UPSC (Union Public Service Commission), HCS (Haryana Civil Services), and various other competitive examinations.

Established with the mission to provide top-tier coaching and guidance for aspiring civil servants, Genious IAS has rapidly emerged as a trusted name for aspirants across Haryana.

  • Structured curriculum for UPSC & HCS aspirants
  • Coaching for SSC, NDA, IPS, UGC NET, RRB & more
  • Personalised, doubt-clearing, mentor-led approach
Discover Genious IAS
School of Humanities

A One-Stop Foundation for School & Competitive Success

Genious IAS's School of Humanities offers comprehensive coaching not only for the UPSC and HCS exams but also for foundational courses for Class 11th & 12th students, making it a one-stop solution for academic and competitive exam preparation.

Students preparing for board exams alongside CLAT and CUET receive dedicated, humanities-focused instruction under the same trusted academic umbrella.

  • Class 11th & 12th Humanities/Arts schooling
  • Integrated competitive-exam preparation
  • CLAT & CUET foundation batches
