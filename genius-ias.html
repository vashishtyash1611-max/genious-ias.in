Introduction

Preparing Rohtak's Future Civil Servants

Genious IAS is a premier coaching institute located in Rohtak, specialising in preparing students for the UPSC (Union Public Service Commission), HCS (Haryana Civil Services), and various other competitive examinations.

With its outstanding faculty and commitment to quality education, Genious IAS has become a top choice for aspirants looking to crack prestigious exams like UPSC, HCS, CLAT, and CUET.

Academic Philosophy

How We Prepare Our Aspirants

A methodical, mentor-driven approach to civil services preparation.

Strong Subject Knowledge

Structured coverage of General Studies, optional subjects, and current affairs with a focus on conceptual clarity.

Regular Practice & Testing

Prelims and Mains test series for continuous evaluation, alongside dedicated essay-writing practice.

Doubt-Clearing Sessions

Frequent, focused sessions ensuring every concept is thoroughly understood before moving ahead.

Personality Development

Mock interviews and personality development sessions to prepare aspirants for the final stage of selection.
