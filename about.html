Institute Overview

Rohtak's Trusted Name in Civil Services & Humanities Education

Genious IAS is a premier coaching institute located in Rohtak, specialising in preparing students for the UPSC (Union Public Service Commission), HCS (Haryana Civil Services), and various other competitive examinations.

Alongside its civil services coaching, the institute's School of Humanities offers foundational courses for Class 11th & 12th students, making Genious IAS a complete academic destination — from school education to civil service aspirations — under one roof.

Our Foundation

Mission, Vision & Philosophy

Our Mission

To provide top-tier coaching and guidance for aspiring civil servants and students, empowering them with the knowledge and confidence to succeed.

Our Vision

To emerge as a beacon of excellence in competitive exam coaching, producing successful candidates who go on to serve the nation with distinction.

Teaching Philosophy

A holistic approach built on strong subject knowledge, regular practice, doubt-clearing sessions, and individual guidance for every student.

Academic Approach

Comprehensive study material, regular testing, current affairs sessions, and mentorship that adapts to each student's pace and goals.
