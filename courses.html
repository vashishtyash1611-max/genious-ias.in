Course FAQs

What batch timings are available?
Genious IAS offers weekday, weekend, and late-evening batches to accommodate school students and working professionals.
Are fee concessions available?
Fee concessions may be available for SC/ST/OBC/Minority/Rural and North-Eastern State students. Please contact us for details.
Can I enrol in both branches?
Yes — school students preparing for CLAT/CUET can also access relevant civil services foundation modules where applicable. Speak to our counsellors for a tailored plan.
How do I register?
Simply fill out the registration form on our Contact page, and our counselling team will reach out to guide you through enrolment.
