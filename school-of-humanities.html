Introduction

A Complete Academic & Competitive Foundation

Genious IAS's School of Humanities offers comprehensive coaching not only for the UPSC and HCS exams but also for foundational courses for Class 11th & 12th students, making it a one-stop solution for all academic and competitive exam preparation needs.

Students receive board-aligned humanities instruction while simultaneously building the foundation needed for CLAT, CUET, and future civil services aspirations.

Academic Philosophy

Balancing Board Success With Future Goals

Board-Aligned Curriculum

Structured coverage of Class 11th & 12th Humanities/Arts subjects aligned to board examination requirements.

CLAT & CUET Integration

Parallel preparation tracks for CLAT and CUET, so students don't have to choose between board and competitive success.

Early Foundation for Civil Services

An early introduction to civil services thinking, current affairs, and analytical skills from the school stage itself.

Individual Attention

Smaller, focused batches with dedicated attention to each student's academic pace and requirements.
